The titles, summaries and reasons shown here are generated by a model from public sources and may be wrong; check the original for anything important. Copyright in the originals stays with each source. The full text is shown only where the source allows it; otherwise only the summary and a link to the original.

## 2. Corrections and takedowns for sources

If you run a source and want something corrected, taken down or shown differently, contact us through the feedback page and we will handle it promptly.
