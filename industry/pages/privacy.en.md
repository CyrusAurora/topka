Page intro:

> About, the terms and the feedback page work without signing in; your Today, past issues, chat and tracking projects need an account and stay with it. We only process what these features need, and we do not sell personal information.

## 1. Account

When you register we store your email address and an irreversible hash of your password (never the password itself). After you sign in, your browser holds a session cookie that identifies you. Registration and sign-in are rate-limited by network address and by account to prevent abuse.

## 2. Your tracking projects, chats and daily 3

What you say in chat, the model's replies, the tracking projects you confirm, the 3 items fixed each day and which of them you opened are stored on the server, belong only to your account, and are used to build your Today and past issues.

## 3. Your own model key and reminder feed address

A model key you enter in Settings is stored encrypted, and the page shows only its last four characters. Chats use it to call the model service you entered, so your chat content is sent to that service and its privacy policy applies. The daily reminder feed address carries a random string that belongs only to you; once you turn it off, the old address stops working at once.

## 4. Feedback

What you submit on the feedback page, the optional email address, the address of the page you were on and any screenshot you choose to attach are stored on the server to handle your feedback. To prevent abuse, the server keeps an irreversible identifier computed from your network address and browser type, used for rate limits and to block abusive sources.

## 5. Data in your browser

Your theme preference and feedback drafts stay in your current browser and are never sent to the server.

## 6. Server and network logs

By default this software does no visitor analytics. Your server, reverse proxy or CDN may keep access logs under their own configuration (such as IP address, time, page address and browser information). State here the services you actually use and how long logs are kept.

## 7. Third-party content

This site shows summaries of and links to third-party originals. When you follow a link to an original, you are on that site and its privacy policy applies.

## 8. Contact and deletion

State how to reach you, and how to look up, correct or delete an account, chats, projects and feedback.
