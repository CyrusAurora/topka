# Trademark and name use

The code in this repository is licensed under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). **The license covers
the code, not the name.** This page is the short version of what that means in practice.

## What you may do

- **Use the name factually.** Say that your site or product "is built on Topka", or "uses Topka",
  and link to this repository. Truthful reference is always fine.
- **Keep the name in a fork you are not distributing.** Fork freely; nothing here restricts that.
- **Name it Topka when you deploy it for yourself or your organisation internally.** Nobody is going
  to police a private deployment.

## What you may not do

- **Do not use the name or logo as the name of your own distributed product or service.** If you run
  a public site built on this code, give it its own name.
- **Do not suggest endorsement or affiliation.** No "official", no logo in your marketing, no
  implication that the maintainers reviewed or approved what you built.

## Why this matters here

`TOPKA` is already registered as a trademark in some classes by unrelated parties, in at least one
jurisdiction. This project claims no trademark rights in the name and cannot grant any. If you want a
name you can build a brand on, use your own — the code does not care what the site is called.

## Changing the name is supported

The site name, its copy and its logo are configuration, not code: they live in `industry/`
(`industry/site.ts` and `industry/brand/`). Renaming a deployment is part of the documented
customisation path (`docs/customize.md`), so nothing here creates a practical obstacle.

## Reporting a misuse

If you believe someone is using the name in a way that implies endorsement, open an issue and
describe it. We cannot litigate, but we can and will say publicly which projects are not ours.
