# Trademark and name use

The MIT License covers the code, not the name.

## You may

- Say that your site or product "is built on Topka" or "uses Topka", and link to this repository.
- Keep the name in a private fork or an internal deployment.

## You may not

- Use the name or logo as the name of your own public product or service. Give a public site its
  own name.
- Suggest endorsement or affiliation: no "official", no logo in your marketing.

`TOPKA` is registered as a trademark in some classes by unrelated parties, and this project claims
no trademark rights in it. To rename a deployment, change `industry/site.ts` and `industry/brand/`
(see `docs/customize.md`).

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue and describe it.
